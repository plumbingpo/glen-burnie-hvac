Kitchen Climate Control

Comprehensive Food-Service HVAC Systems

In a restaurant, hood exhaust, makeup air, and dining-room comfort are all connected, and a system that's undersized or failing shows up first as a sweltering cook line and complaints at the tables. Fryers, ranges, and grills pour enormous heat loads into the space, while the hoods pull conditioned air straight out of the building. We handle rooftop package units, split systems, tonnage and airflow sizing, and makeup air balancing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Many restaurant comfort complaints trace back to air balance. If a kitchen runs under negative pressure, it pulls dining-room air in the wrong direction, sucks hot or cold outdoor air in through every door, and wears out your staff at peak service. Our technicians balance the makeup air to the hood exhaust and document every visit in written reports that landlords and auditors can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Every commercial job we take on starts with safety. Our technicians inspect heat exchangers for cracks, verify correct venting and combustion air, test electrical connections and disconnects, verify the refrigerant charge, clear condensate lines, and measure ventilation rates in occupied areas. The goal is straightforward: no carbon monoxide hazards, no poor air quality, no refrigerant leaks, and no ceiling tiles ruined by an overflowing drain. Findings go into a written report that ranks each issue by priority and recommends a specific fix.

Consistent professional service protects the people and property you're responsible for, and acting on expert recommendations keeps minor issues from becoming liabilities. It also creates a record. We maintain service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or auditor asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Fast Response

Fast Commercial HVAC Service

A commercial HVAC failure starts costing money the minute it happens. Kitchens get too hot to work, shoppers walk out, server rooms overheat, and tenants start complaining. Our commercial line gets a technician rolling fast, anywhere in Glen Burnie and the surrounding area.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: we handle all of it on commercial equipment. The priority is diagnosing the fault and getting heating or cooling back fast. Once it's running, we put maintenance and monitoring in place so it doesn't happen again, and every response comes with full documentation for your facility files.

Maintenance Programs

Commercial Scheduled Maintenance Programs

A scheduled maintenance program from Glen Burnie HVAC is built around your property's specific equipment, how the space is used, and your budget. On each scheduled visit, we handle inspections, coil cleaning, condensate line clearing, filter changes at the right MERV rating, belt and bearing checks, refrigerant charge and static pressure testing, burner and heat exchanger inspection, and seasonal prep ahead of peak demand. Nothing controls commercial HVAC costs better than preventing failures in the first place.

Next to an emergency replacement, scheduled maintenance costs a fraction as much, and it sharply cuts the risk of downtime and tenant complaints. Businesses that commit to a program typically see fewer outages, longer equipment life, and lower energy bills. Call us to talk through a plan for your property and budget.
